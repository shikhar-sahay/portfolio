## DEVICE VETTING RULE (binding for the section-by-section pass)

The portfolio is intentionally responsive and dynamic across device classes, never a desktop layout that merely does not break on phones. Every section touched during the website-wide pass is vetted at large desktop, normal laptop, narrower desktop, tablet, and phone widths (representative: 1440, 1280, 1024, 820, 390, plus one narrower phone where practical), judging reading order, typography, wrapping, spacing, scale, placement, timeline readability, touch behavior, links, hierarchy, and visual balance, not just overflow. Interactions adapt to input: mouse and trackpad may use hover, proximity, and tilt; touch gets intentional alternatives with natural scrolling preserved; keyboard keeps every interactive element accessible with a restrained response where applicable; reduced motion always gets a coherent static equivalent.
